Full room resets on Long Island

The room you've been avoiding — garage, basement, playroom or home office — reset top to bottom into a space you'll love walking into.

For the spaces that got away from you

Every home has one: the room where things go "for now" and stay for years. A reset means we take the whole room apart — every shelf, bin and corner — declutter it with you, deep-organize what stays, and rebuild the space with zones and systems so it actually functions. It's the most dramatic transformation we offer, and the one clients tell us changed how their whole house feels.

Rooms we reset

  • Garages — park the car inside again, with tools, sports gear and seasonal items in zones.
  • Basements — from catch-all storage to a space your family actually uses.
  • Playrooms — toy rotation and kid-height storage that children can maintain.
  • Home offices — paper systems, cable sanity and a desk you can actually work at.
  • Kitchens, pantries & closets — when they need more than a tune-up.

Real resets, real results

A playroom in Islip that went from cluttered to calm. A full kitchen and pantry reset in Massapequa. A primary bedroom in Garden City. See the photos in our before & after gallery — every project shown is a real Long Island home.

Resets usually take one to three sessions depending on the room. We serve all of Nassau and Suffolk Counties, and we can combine a reset with decluttering or space optimization in adjacent rooms.
