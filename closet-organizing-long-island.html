Closet organizing on Long Island

Closets that close, clothes you can actually see, and a morning routine that doesn't start with a pile on the bed. Hands-on closet organizing, with zero judgment.

Organizing, not carpentry

Most closets don't need a new built-in system — they need editing and a plan. We work with the closet you already have: we take everything out, help you decide what still fits your life, and put the rest back so the things you wear most are the easiest to reach. If a few bins, matching hangers or a shelf divider would make a real difference, we'll recommend them.

Closets we organize

  • Primary walk-in closets — shared closets split so each person has their own zone.
  • Reach-in bedroom closets — small spaces where every inch has to earn its place.
  • Kids' closets — set up at their height, with room to grow into the next size.
  • Linen and hall closets — towels, sheets and cleaning supplies you can find in seconds.
  • Coat and entry closets — seasonal rotation so winter gear isn't fighting summer gear.

How a closet session works

It starts with a free 20-minute consultation. A single closet usually takes one session of 3–5 hours: we empty it, sort with you into keep, donate and discard, group what stays by how you use it, and label anything that isn't obvious. You'll see a real before and after of a bedroom closet in our gallery.

We organize closets across Nassau County and Suffolk County. If the closet is part of a bigger project, it pairs well with decluttering or space optimization for the rest of the house.
