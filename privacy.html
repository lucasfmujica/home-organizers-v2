Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 6, 2026. Your privacy matters to us — here's exactly what we collect and how we use it.

Who we are

Home Organizers Long Island ("we", "us") provides home and workspace organizing services across Long Island, NY. This website is homeorganizersli.com. You can reach us at homeorganizerslongisland@gmail.com or (415) 963-2571.

Information we collect

When you submit our contact form, we collect the information you provide: your name, email address, phone number (optional), the type of help you're looking for, and your message. Separately, our analytics tools collect anonymous usage data — see "Cookies and analytics" below. We do not collect any other personal information through this website.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your inquiry and schedule your free consultation.
  • To communicate with you about your project before, during and after our work together.

We will never sell, rent or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

How your data is processed and stored

Our contact form is processed by Netlify Forms, our website hosting provider. Form submissions are stored securely by Netlify and forwarded to our email. You can read Netlify's privacy policy at netlify.com/privacy.

Cookies and analytics

We use two analytics tools to understand how people find and use this site, so we can keep improving it. We do not run advertising trackers, and we never sell or share what these tools collect.

  • Google Analytics 4 — measures how many people visit, which pages they read, and which sources (search, social, referrals) bring them here. See Google's privacy policy.
  • Microsoft Clarity — records anonymised page interactions (clicks and scrolling) so we can spot confusing layouts. See Microsoft's privacy statement.

On your first visit you'll see a banner asking whether you accept analytics cookies. Until you accept, Google Analytics runs in a cookieless mode that only records anonymous, aggregated visit counts, and Microsoft Clarity does not load at all. If you decline, nothing further is stored on your device.

To change your choice later, clear this site's data in your browser settings, or reset your cookie preferences to see the banner again.

Data retention

We keep contact form submissions only as long as needed to serve you. You may request a copy of your data or ask us to delete it at any time by emailing homeorganizerslongisland@gmail.com.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the email above and we will respond promptly.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.

Questions about your privacy?

We're happy to help — just reach out.
