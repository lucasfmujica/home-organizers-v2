Home organizing in the Hamptons

Summer homes ready before you arrive, closed up calmly when the season ends, and year-round homes that finally feel easy to live in — with warm, hands-on help and zero judgment.

A second home shouldn't feel like a second job

A house that's only lived in part of the year collects a special kind of clutter: beach gear from three summers ago, guest rooms that turned into storage, closets packed for a season that already ended. We help Hamptons homeowners get their houses organized around how they actually use them — so arriving feels like a vacation, not a project.

Opening your summer home

Before the season starts we set the house up for the months ahead: summer clothes unpacked and organized, pantry and kitchen in order, guest rooms ready for visitors, and beach, pool and kids' gear sorted into places that are easy to grab and easy to put back.

Closing it for the winter

When the summer ends we help you close up calmly: summer items edited and stored in labeled bins, closets and kitchen cleared of what doesn't need to stay, and everything put away so that opening the house next year is quick and easy.

Year-round homes, too

Not every Hamptons home is a summer house. For full-time residents we do the same work we do across Long Island — decluttering & downsizing, closet organizing, space optimization and full room resets for basements, garages and playrooms.

Towns we serve on the East End

Westhampton, Southampton, Water Mill, Bridgehampton, Sagaponack, Sag Harbor, East Hampton, Amagansett and Montauk — and the rest of Suffolk County. If you're on the East End, we come to you.

How it works

It starts with a free 20-minute consultation, where we listen to how you use the house and what you'd like it to feel like. A single closet or pantry usually takes one session of 3–5 hours; opening or closing a whole house depends on its size, and you'll get a realistic estimate before we start.
