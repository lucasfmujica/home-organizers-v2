Questions

Do you organize holiday decorations in the garage or basement? expand_more

Yes. We help you sort decorations, pack them into labeled storage, and set up a garage or basement system you can actually use. Larger projects like garages and basements typically take one to three sessions. You get a realistic time estimate in the free 20-minute consultation.

Should I book before Christmas or in January? expand_more

Both can work, and they are different jobs. Before mid-December the focus is making space and getting decorations out. From late December through January we help you pack everything away and reset the garage or basement.

Do I need to buy bins before the session? expand_more

Please don't prepare or shop first unless we ask you to. We need to see the space as you live in it. If something is missing, we can talk about it during the consultation.

What areas do you serve? expand_more

We serve all of Long Island (Nassau County, Suffolk County and the Hamptons), New York City, and the rest of New York State.

How long does a garage or basement project take? expand_more

Larger projects like garages, basements or full room resets typically take one to three sessions. A realistic estimate comes with your free consultation.
